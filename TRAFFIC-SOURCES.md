# Traffic-inspired level progression

The game uses a curated sequence of scenarios from off-peak streets to severe bottlenecks. This is not a measured, official or live ranking of neighbourhoods. Conditions depend on route, direction, incidents and time of day. The minimum move count determines game difficulty, not an invented traffic statistic.

1. Ikoyi (off-peak), 3 moves
2. Yaba, 5 moves
3. Surulere, 7 moves
4. Ikeja, 9 moves
5. Maryland, 11 moves
6. Ojuelegba, 13 moves
7. Third Mainland Bridge, 15 moves
8. Lekki–Ajah, 17 moves
9. Oshodi, 19 moves
10. Apapa, 21 moves

Sources consulted October 16, 2026:

- Lagos Traffic Radio: free movement on the Lekki–Ikoyi Link Bridge in an August 12, 2026 report. This supports the off-peak opening scenario, not a claim that Ikoyi is always clear. https://trafficradio961.ng/lekki-peninsula-am-traffic-update-4/
- Nigerian Shippers’ Council: firsthand commute reports describe traffic at Ikeja crossroads, Iyana Ipaja–Oshodi and Apapa–Ijora; explains Apapa's heavy trucks and port infrastructure. https://shipperscouncil.gov.ng/traffic-report/
- LAMATA project report: documents the Ikeja–Maryland–Ojuelegba corridor. https://www.lamata-ng.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Final-LBR-Project-Report.pdf
- Lagos Traffic Radio: Gbagada/Oworonshoki incident demonstrates pressure on Third Mainland Bridge approaches. An incident example, not typical travel-time data. https://trafficradio961.ng/%F0%9F%9A%A8-flash-update-gbagada-oworonshoki-expressway/
- Lagos Traffic Radio: mixed flow and slow sections on the Lekki–Ajah corridor. https://trafficradio961.ng/traffic-update-lekki-ajah-expressway-axis/
- The Guardian, July 13, 2026: recurring truck and tanker gridlock on Mile 2–Apapa and Tin Can corridors. https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/metro/motorists-lament-as-mile-2-apapa-gridlock-persists-eight-years-after-presidential-intervention/

The exact placement of the middle levels is a game-design judgment. Yaba and Surulere serve as introductory urban junction scenarios rather than claims of lower congestion than every later area.
